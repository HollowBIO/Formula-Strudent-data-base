# Sponsorship Points

Points for the partnership team, pulled from the vault on 2026-11-10. The two biggest asks are an Ecumaster EMU Black ECU (about €1,000) and a limited-slip differential from Torsen, TRE or Drexler (about $1,000–2,800). All prices are research estimates from the concept notes, not quotes.

## The pitch

- First-year combustion (CV) Formula Student car for the 2026–27 season, built cheap, simple and reliable.
- Target events: FS Romania (Speed Park, Bacău), FS Balkans (Romania) and FS Poland (Autodrom Słomczyn). All three use the common Formula Student rules.
- CV places are limited: 15 at FS Balkans and 10 at FS Poland (2026 numbers). FS Romania also has a Concept Class for teams without a finished car.
- Car highlights: 700 cc-class twin engine with a 20 mm air restrictor, steel tube spaceframe, double-wishbone pushrod suspension, high-downforce aero (front and rear wings, sidepods), and our own CAN data logger.
- Logo space: bodywork gets vinyl instead of paint, for colours and sponsor logos. Wings, endplates, sidepods and nose are all visible surfaces.
- Skills: team members are training in TIG welding, composites (carbon and fibreglass), CAD and CNC. That is a recruiting pipeline for engineering sponsors.
- Idea: the team's first car is being restored (for СММ). It could also be a display car at sponsor events.
