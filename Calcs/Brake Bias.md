# Brake Bias

Feeds [[Brakes]].

## Ideal front bias at deceleration $a$ (in g)

Front axle load under braking:
$$W_f = W\left(\frac{b}{L} + a\frac{h}{L}\right)$$
where $b$ = CG to rear axle, $L$ = wheelbase, $h$ = CG height.

Ideal front bias = $W_f / W$.
