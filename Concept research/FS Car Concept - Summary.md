# FS Car Concept – Summary

First-year combustion Formula Student car, aimed at FS Romania, FS Balkans and FS Poland; built cheap, simple and reliable, with aero for team morale.

## Competitions

- Common FS rules; combustion rules stable 2025–2026
- CV slots: Balkans 15, Poland 10; Romania has a CV class
- Details: [[FS Competitions|FS Competitions – Romania vs Balkans vs Poland CV focus]]

## Engine

- Plan A: existing Honda Transalp XL700V (680 cc V-twin) if it runs in a few weekends for under about $1,000
- Plan B: Yamaha CP2 (689 cc parallel twin)
- 20 mm restrictor, plenum needed, split sidepod radiators, naturally aspirated (no turbo year one)
- Noise: 110 dB(C) limit; used slip-on silencer to start
- Details: [[Engine Decision|Engine Decision]], [[Noise Test and Silencer|Noise Test and Silencer]]

## Chassis and suspension

- Steel tube spaceframe; existing 27 × 2 mm frame may fail hoop rules (to check)
- Double wishbones, pushrod front and rear, four corner springs, ARBs front and rear
- 13" OZ Racing 7J wheels, Hoosier R25B 20.5 × 7.0-13 (age to check)
- Details: [[Suspension - First Car Concept|Suspension – First Car Concept]], [[To Check|To Check]]

## Drivetrain

- Chain drive
- Differential: open question; need a proper LSD (no spool, no open diff, no own build)
- Details: [[Differential|Differential – Open Question]]

## Aero

- High downforce: front and rear wings, sidepods; floor deferred
- Bolt-on, removable; foam-core carbon wings, printed plugs and lost cores for bodywork
- Details: [[Aero Concept|Aero Concept – First Car]]

## Brakes and steering

- Two hydraulic circuits with balance bar; one disc per wheel; metric parts only; no ABS
- Existing calipers and master cylinders unidentified
- Rack and pinion, adjustable Ackermann; no FDM-printed parts in steering, brakes or suspension

## Electronics

- Ecumaster EMU Black ECU
- Own microcontroller CAN logger: steering angle, 4 wheel speeds, g-forces, pedals, airspeed
- Details: [[Electronics|Electronics – ECU and Data Logger]]
