# Suspension Concept – Typical FS Setup (Steel Tube Frame)

## Team decisions

- Frame: steel tube spaceframe
- Wheels: **13-inch rims**

## Typical FS layout

- Double unequal-length wishbones, front and rear (multi-link is rarely used)
- Pushrod or pullrod to rockers, inboard dampers (Ohlins TTX25 is the common choice)
- Anti-roll bars front and rear (U-bar or T-bar with adjustable blades)
- Rear toe link
- Third (heave) spring: used by some top, aero-heavy teams to control pitch and ride height; usually skipped on first-year cars

## Typical geometry (starting values)

- Wheelbase: about 1,530–1,600 mm (rules minimum 1,525 mm)
- Track: about 1,200 mm, rear slightly narrower
- Ride height: about 30–40 mm
- Static camber: about −1 to −2.5°
- Caster: about 3–6°
- Kingpin inclination: about 4–8°, small scrub radius
- Roll centre: about 0–30 mm front, slightly higher rear
- Anti-dive / anti-squat: about 10–30%

## Springs and roll

- Motion ratio: close to 1:1
- Ride frequency: about 3–4.5 Hz
- Roll gradient: about 1–1.5° per g

## Notes

- Values are typical starting points, not rules requirements
