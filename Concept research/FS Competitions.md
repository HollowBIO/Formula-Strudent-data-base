# FS Competitions – Balkans vs Poland (CV focus)

## FS Balkans (FSBK)

- Separate event, also held in Romania
- 2026 slots: 15 CV (incl. hybrid), 15 EV, 5 CV/EV alumni vehicles
- No driverless class

FS Czech  (plan B)

## Key takeaways for a CV team

- All three follow the common FS Rules, with event handbook additions
- Most CV slots: Balkans (15); fewest: Poland (10)
- Combustion rules stable 2025–2026; FS Rules 2027 still include a full CV section
