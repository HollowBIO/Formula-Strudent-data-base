# Suspension – First Car Concept

First car: double wishbones with pushrods at both axles, four corner springs, anti-roll bars front and rear, 13-inch wheels on a steel tube frame.

## Proposed (not yet agreed by the team)

- Frame: steel tube spaceframe
- Wheels: 13-inch rims
- Layout: double unequal-length wishbones plus a rear toe link (no multi-link)
- Actuation: pushrod front and rear, dampers mounted high for track access
- Springs: four corner springs; no third (heave) spring in year one
- Anti-roll bars: front and rear, adjustable blades
- Frame and suspension mounts rated for aero loads from day one, so wings can bolt on later

## Why not multi-link

- More freedom to set camber, toe, roll centre and anti-squat independently, with simple links that only take tension or compression
- A wishbone plus toe link is already effectively five links, so the extra gain is small
- Costs more parts, more frame pickup points, harder kinematics

## Pushrod vs pullrod

- Pushrod: in compression, rocker high; room above the driver's legs at the front, above the gearbox at the rear; easy damper access
- Pullrod: in tension, so a thinner, lighter rod; slightly lower centre of gravity; cleaner upper area for aero
- Pullrod downsides: poor access, tight low packaging, loads into the lower frame
- Some teams run pullrod at the rear only when the engine leaves room; revisit for a later car
