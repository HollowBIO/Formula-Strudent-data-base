# Differential – Open Question and Options

Open question: no diff chosen yet; discuss the options below with the team. Final drive stays chain (Transalp has a chain sprocket output).

## Requirements agreed

- No accepting understeer in tight corners: FS tracks have lots of hairpins, so a spool or locked quad diff is out as the final solution
- Open diff is effectively out too: the inside rear spins on exit and drive is lost
- Need a proper limited-slip (Torsen/ATB or clutch type)
- Do not build our own diff or housing: too risky for reliability for a new team

## Options (cheapest proper option first)

| Option | Rough cost | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Used FS diff from another team (Drexler / TRE) | often about half of new | about 3–4 kg | Best value; ask Polish, Czech, Romanian teams |
| Lada classic + aftermarket LSD insert (plate or helical) | cheap | about 15–25 kg in housing | Original housing keeps bearings and seals right; only a sprocket adapter and mounts needed; heavy |
| Road-car diff in original housing (BMW, MX-5, clutch LSD) | about $100–400 used | about 15–25 kg | Factory LSD versions rare; heavy and raises the CG |
| TRE Quaife ATB (Mk2/Mk3/Mk4), new | up to about $1,950 | about 4 kg (Mk2 with bearings and tripods) | Complete, plug-and-play, rebuild without removing the chain |
| Torsen T-1 University Special, new | about $1,000–1,500 diff (estimate); about $2,000–2,800 delivered with housings, shipping, duty | about 3–4 kg | Student pricing; ask Torsen for a quote |
| Drexler FS, new | about $2,000+ | about 3–4 kg | Most adjustable; sponsor or discount route |

## Ruled out (and why)

- Spool / locked axle: mid-corner understeer in tight hairpins
- Open diff: loses drive on exit
- Quad diffs: CFMoto rear units are locked bevels; Polaris front units are on-demand clutch, not LSD; others are open or lockable
- Smart diff: built into the transaxle; open diff
- Viscous LSD: slow, fades with heat over endurance
- Own diff or custom housing: gear design and precision machining beyond a new team; reliability risk
- Whole damaged quad as a donor: one listing about 437,000 UAH (lightly damaged Polaris 450), and the diff type is wrong anyway

## Fallback if nothing is found in time

- Spool for testing only, with the rear designed so a proper diff can bolt in later
- Reduce spool push with a narrower rear track, stiffer rear roll stiffness, and driver technique
