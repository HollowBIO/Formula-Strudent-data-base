# D7 Aero

## Requirements / go criteria
- Bolt-on and removable: the car must always run without it
- Own small group with a go/no-go date; final call depends on team workload
- Weight (~10–20 kg full package) reassessed once parts exist

## Options
| Option                            | Status              | Notes                                          |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| No aero                           | fallback            |                                                |
| Wings (2–3 elements) + sidepods   | candidate           | ~1–2 s/lap for good FS packages; team morale   |
| Full package incl. floor/diffuser | rejected (deferred) | Ride-height sensitive; sump/exhaust in the way |

Manufacturing: foam-core carbon wings, printed plugs/lost cores for bodywork.

## Recommendation
Wings + sidepods; keep the underside clear so a floor stays possible.
possible damage in crash goes up
