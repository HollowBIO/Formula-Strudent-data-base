# D1.4 Intake plenum

A plenum is required (throttle → 20 mm restrictor → plenum → runner per cylinder). Open: volume and how to make it.

## Requirements / go criteria
- Start at ~2–4 L for a 700 cc twin, tune by testing
- Even flow to both cylinders (check with CFD)
- Leak-free: engine must stall almost immediately when the intake is blocked at scrutineering
- Survives vibration, heat, backfires; bracket-supported
- Doesn't raise the CG (tightest on the Transalp V)

## Options (manufacturing)
| Option | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon, lost-core layup | candidate | Strong, heat-tolerant |
| Heat-resistant 3D print | candidate | Fast to iterate |
