# D1-O1 Transalp XL700V (Plan A)

680 cc V-twin the team already owns; has sat on a wooden plate for ~6 years.

## Pros
- Already owned: cheapest path (~$500–1,800 incl. ECU)
- Flatter low-end torque; needs less air at peak power (~0.047 kg/s vs restrictor choke at ~0.07 kg/s)

## Cons
- Unknown condition
- Engine-only: harness, ECU, injectors, fuel pump, throttle bodies missing
- Only ~10 listings in Ukraine — hard to get a spare
- V layout makes the plenum harder to package
