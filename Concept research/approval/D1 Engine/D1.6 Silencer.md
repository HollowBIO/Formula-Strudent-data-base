# D1.6 Silencer

## Requirements / go criteria
- ≤ 103 dB(C) at idle, ≤ 110 dB(C) up to test speed (fast weighting, 0.5 m, 45°)
- Outlet ≤ 45 cm behind the rear axle

## Options
| Option | Status | Mass | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Used aftermarket slip-on | candidate | ~2 kg | Cheap start |
| Stock Transalp silencer | rejected | ~4–6 kg | Too heavy |
| Own straight-through build | candidate | — | Repack every season |
| Used FS-team silencer | candidate | — | Already sized for a restricted engine |

## Recommendation
Slip-on for testing; keep it if well under 110 dB(C), otherwise lengthen/pack or build our own.
