# D1.5 Cooling layout

## Question
One radiator or two in the sidepods?

## Requirements / go criteria
- Fans do most of the cooling at FS speeds
- Sidepods are shaped around the radiators ([[D7 Aero]])

## Options
| Option | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single front radiator (stock) | candidate | Simplest |
| Split: one radiator per sidepod | candidate | Y-split hot line, rejoin before pump; equal-length hoses; bleed at highest point; one fan each; ~an afternoon of fabrication |

## Recommendation
Split sidepod radiators; the stock pump is usually enough.
