# FS Competitions – Romania vs Balkans vs Poland (CV focus)

## Formula Student Romania (FSRo)

- Venue: Speed Park, Bacău, Romania
- 2026: 4th edition, 11–16 August 2026
- Rules: Formula Student 2026 Regulations, with event-specific exceptions in the FSRo Handbook
- Classes: accepts combustion (CV) and electric (EV) teams; also has a Concept Class (C2) for teams without a car

## FS Balkans (FSBK)

- Separate event, also held in Romania
- 2026 slots: 15 CV (incl. hybrid), 15 EV, 5 CV/EV alumni vehicles
- No driverless class

## Formula Student Poland (FSP)

- Venue: Autodrom Słomczyn
- Official FSG-recommended edition since 2025
- 2026 classes: EV and CV(&HY)
- 2026 slots: 30 total, 20 EV and 10 CV&HY, so CV places are limited

## Key takeaways for a CV team

- All three follow the common FS Rules, with event handbook additions
- Most CV slots: Balkans (15); fewest: Poland (10)
- Combustion rules stable 2025–2026; FS Rules 2027 still include a full CV section
