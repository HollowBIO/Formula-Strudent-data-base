## 2027 check — 13 Nov 2026

**Changed in the 2027 rules** (listed in the rulebook changelog):
- Aero heights: front devices <350 mm ahead of the front tyres (the old row said 250 mm); devices between the front tyres and head restraint must be below the tyre tops (the old sidepod row said 500 mm).
- Aero widths: 700–1100 mm may go out to the rear tyres; tyre top to 700 mm must stay 150 mm inboard of the rear tyres.
- Harness: FHR replaces HANS; 50 mm shoulder straps and FHR-specific mount spacing allowed.
- Head restraint padding: SFI 45.2 or FIA list 17 type A/B.
- Pedal box must be securely mounted with no excessive deflection.
- Steering must not deform excessively under driver load.
- BSPD: each sensor signal must be separately breakable for inspection.
- Pneumatic systems: certification only needed above 10 bar; shielding rules unified.
- Final drive mounting: critical fasteners, except unmodified OEM parts.
- Critical fasteners on the chassis must not be below the rollover envelope.
- Jacks: driven wheels ≥75 mm off the ground.
- Catch cans and LV battery: "securely mounted" (no longer mounted to the chassis).

**Fixed or added from the 2027 text:**
- IA bolts are T3.17.5, not T3.17.2.
- A dammed single reservoir is allowed for the brakes.
- Missing details: nose 38 mm radius, sump skid guard, fan power ≤500 W, intake manifold mounting, number plate format, extinguisher type.
